Supported formats

The formats the reel and the PDF read, and the models CloneBuster renders.

CloneBuster records and timestamps files of any format. This page lists the formats it also makes a preview of: the image a reel and Proof.pdf show.

Formats the reel reads

FormatPreview
PNG, JPGThe full image.
PSDThe flattened composite image, 8-bit RGB or grayscale. Photoshop saves it only with Maximize compatibility on. 16-bit, CMYK and PSB files aren't read.
Aseprite (.ase, .aseprite)Every frame on one sheet, with its visible layers merged. Blend modes other than normal and tilemap layers aren't read.
Krita (.kra)The merged image Krita saves in the file.
Blender (.blend) outside Assets, or inside it without Blender installedThe thumbnail Blender saves in the file. Compressed files aren't read.
Procreate (.procreate)The thumbnail Procreate saves in the file, at low resolution.
Models in AssetsA render made after Unity imports the version. Refer to Model previews.

Model previews

When Unity imports a new version of a model in Assets, CloneBuster renders it at once, in the Editor, and records the render as that version's preview.

PropertyValue
FormatsFBX, OBJ, glTF and GLB (with a glTF importer), Collada, and .blend with Blender installed.
CameraA fixed angle, the same for every version, framed on that version's bounds.
PoseThe pose the file holds: animation doesn't play.
Level of detailLOD0 only.
Size1024 by 1024 pixels.

Models outside Assets aren't rendered: Unity can't import them.

What Proof.pdf shows

Kind of fileProof.pdf shows
Formats the reel readsThe image at the first and the latest ticked version, beside the copy's screenshot if you added one.
Code and text (.cs, JSON, YAML, scenes, prefabs and other text)Name, hash, dates and version count. A line excerpt only when you enter a range.
Everything else (audio, video, models outside Assets other than .blend)Name, hash, dates and version count.

What CloneBuster doesn't read

The following have no preview, so they can't be shown in a reel or as an image in a PDF. Their versions are still recorded and timestamped like any other file:

  • Audio and video files.
  • Scenes and prefabs.
  • GIMP files (.xcf), which save no merged image.
  • Models outside Assets in a format other than .blend.
  • Files larger than 1 GB.
