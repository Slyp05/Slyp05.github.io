Inspect and debug

Validate members before you enter Play mode, and inspect what injected members hold.

The Selvage section at the top of the Inspector window displays the state of each member of the selected GameObject or asset. The Dependencies window gives the reason for each result, validates the whole project, and displays what each member holds. To open it, go to Tools > Selvage > Dependencies.
