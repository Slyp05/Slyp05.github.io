Concordance window reference

Look up every part of the Concordance window and of its Inspector section.

Member list

To open the Concordance window, go to Tools > Concordance > Open Window. Its help (?) button opens this manual.

The list shows members under their assembly and type. A warning icon marks a member that needs a decision, and an eye marks a watched one: select the eye to watch or stop watching. In Play mode, each row shows the member's value.

ControlDescription
Search fieldFinds any member your code writes, by member, type, assembly, script or object name. Ctrl+F (macOS: Command+F) focuses it.
Watch filterLists all members, the watched ones, or the others.
Access filterLists members by their declared access: public, internal, protected or private.
Modified OnlyLists only the members a script modifies or you watch. Enabled by default.
Needs a DecisionLists only the members that need a decision, with their number.
Collapse All, Expand AllFolds or unfolds every heading.

Right-click a member to watch it for this session, always, or stop watching it.

Member details

A selected member shows its name, its Watch button, and the following sections, each with a warning box for any decision it needs. A special case, such as a member animation drives, says so under the name.

SectionDescription
Modifier OrderThe scripts that modify the member, by phase, in the order they apply. Drag a script to pin it; Unpin returns it to its phase in code; Any Order marks a phase whose order doesn't matter. Read-only in Play mode. With one script, Modifier Lines lists its lines instead.
WritesEvery write to the member in your project other than its modifier lines, grouped by source. Add Reference tracks the writes of an assembly, and Intended marks a script as an intended writer.
ValuesIn Play mode, for each object: the base value, each modifier with its owner, line and the value after it, then the value. A toggle on each modifier leaves it out, in the Editor only. A Conflicting writes row offers Open Line and Mark as Intended.
HistoryIn Play mode, for a watched member, its last 64 changes, each with its frame, who made it, its line, and the value after it.

Inspector section

When you select a GameObject or a ScriptableObject with modified members, the Inspector window shows a Concordance section under its header, folded by default. Its header counts the members per state; select a count to show only those rows. Select a row to open its member in the Concordance window.

StateMeaning
order not set, pin to reviewThe member's order needs a decision.
untrackedAn assembly without the Concordance reference writes the member: add the reference so Concordance tracks its writes.
ordered, one scriptNothing to decide.
conflicting writesIn Play mode, two objects overwrite each other's value.
modified, unmodifiedIn Play mode, whether modifiers apply to the member now.
