## Rules

- Short: the problem, CloneBuster as the answer, the two independent timestamps, the copy without history, the proof. Nothing else.
- No repeat of the cover: no robot, no tagline, no stack of prints, no dashed outlines.
- Each beat starts on a bar of the music (120 BPM, a bar every 2 s).
- The copy is the game itself, unchanged: no invented title or branding.
- Every list of versions is the real Exporter, so the dates read as the authorities' receipts, not as file metadata.
- No claim that an edit history catches AI use, or that a reel shows who or what made something.
- The story ends with the proof and the reel: no clone removed.
- Captions sit on one line, and hold at least 0.3 s per word plus 1 s.

## Music

Bright and punchy, like the store art: saw stabs, an octave bass, a square-wave hook and tight drums in F major. The AI copy and the copy without history sit on a D minor pedal, and a crash opens each section that answers them.
